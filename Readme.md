## 3 Actionable Business Recommendations
1. **Optimize High Discounts:** High discount tiers (`15%–20%`) reduce average profit per order compared to `0%–5%`, so tie deep discounts to higher order quantities or higher-margin items.
2. **Focus on Top Categories:** `Home Decor` and `Furniture` lead in sales and profit, so prioritize stock and promotions there while improving margins on lower-performing categories like `Groceries` and `Beauty`.
3. **Grow Lower-Volume Regions:** `North` leads sales, while `South` and `West` have room for growth using targeted offers and popular payment methods (`UPI`, cards, `COD`).
